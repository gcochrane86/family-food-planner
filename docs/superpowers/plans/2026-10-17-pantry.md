# Phase 7: Pantry Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A working Pantry tab with three sections:
- **Freezer:** batch-made portions.
- **Fridge and cupboard:** staples, plus anything else you've added.
- **Batch-and-freeze recommendations:** for recipes with a make-ahead part that freezes well (pastes, sauces, shredded meat).

Using a frozen portion skips that part of the shopping, but only after you've checked the freezer, before the basket fills. The fridge and cupboard fill up by hand and from delivered Sainsbury's orders.

**Architecture:**
- **A recipe's batchable part** is described on the recipe (`recipes.batch`): its name, the ingredient group it uses, the steps it covers, how many to make, and how long it keeps frozen.
- **Each dinner records a batch plan** (`meal_slots.batch`):
  - `make`: make a batch, so the list buys extra.
  - `made`: the batch went in the freezer.
  - `frozen`: use a frozen portion, not yet checked.
  - `frozen_ok`: the freezer check said it's there, and the portion is taken.
- **The freezer is `prepared_items`.**
- **Delivered orders** are recorded in `pantry_orders`, so each is added once.

**Tech Stack:** React + Vite + TS, Supabase Postgres + RLS, Vitest + PGlite, Deno Edge Function (propose-week), tsx CLIs.

**Spec:** `docs/spec.md`:

## Global Constraints
- **The owner's choices (17 Oct 2026):**
  - The fridge and cupboard fill both by hand and from Sainsbury's orders.
  - Before a basket fill that assumes frozen portions, the household confirms each one is really in the freezer.
  - The fridge and cupboard are trusted as they are: staples marked have / low / out already decide what's bought.
- **Shopping model (owner, 17 Oct 2026):**
  - The two weekly Sainsbury's shops buy fresh meat and vegetables, plus any staple marked out that a recipe needs.
  - The flavour pantry and freezer supply the rest: spices, vinegars, umami, tins, grains and batch pastes.
  - The starting list (an Ethan Chlebowski–style flavour pantry) is already loaded as staples:
    - 58 in all
    - specialty items (miso, fish sauce, tahini and so on) start as out, so the first recipe that uses each one buys it
  - Freezer batches (pastes, stock paste, shredded beef) arrive with this phase.
- **Copy:**
  - Tabs are "Freezer", "Fridge" and "Cupboard".
  - The batch tip is "Make {makes}× and freeze {makes − 1}".
  - The check is headed "Check the freezer first", with the buttons "It's there" and "Not there".
- **Batch defaults:**
  - `makes` is 4 (allowed 2–8).
  - `freezer_days` is 90 (allowed 1–365).
- **Frozen portions:** one portion covers one dinner of that recipe, whatever its portions or guests.
- **Expired portions:** a freezer portion past its use-by date is listed (red, "use or bin") but never planned or counted as available.
- **Migration 0011 holds all the database changes.** The owner runs it before the merge, as with 0009 and 0010.
- **Sainsbury's rule change:** reading **My Account → Orders** (read-only) is allowed only for the new pantry step. Nothing else in My Account changes.

## Review Focus
1. **Two dinners of the same recipe with one frozen portion:** only one gets `frozen`, and the other buys its paste ingredients.
2. **Answering "Not there" after another dinner's "It's there":** the list recomputes at once. The Sainsbury's buttons stay disabled until no `frozen` slots remain.
3. **A scheduled basket fill while the freezer check is unanswered:** `basket list` refuses with "Check the freezer in the app first", so the run finishes as failed with that reason, and nothing is bought on a guess.
4. **A batch whose step titles no longer exist after an edit:** the recipe card shows no tip on those steps, but shopping still uses the batch's ingredient group. Nothing crashes.
5. **A `make` dinner with guests or extra portions:** the batch group is bought at `factor × makes`. The rest of the recipe is bought at `factor` only.
