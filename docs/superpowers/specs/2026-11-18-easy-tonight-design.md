# Easy tonight and keep-one-in tins (short design)

**Why:** on a night nothing's planned, find a dinner that can be made from the cupboard plus a five-minute walk to the
Spar. Time matters less than ease. Keep the cupboard stocked with long-life tins so that works.

**Goals**
- A chip, "Easy tonight", on the Recipes tab. It shows dinners where everything not in the pantry is a Spar basic
  (everyday things any Spar always has).
- Each card says one of these:
  - "All in the cupboard"
  - "From the Spar: …"
  - "Skip if they've none: …", for fresh herbs and chilli, which are garnishes or have a dried swap
- One tap adds the Spar items to the Spar list (Shop).
- Order: one-pan and foil-bag dinners first, then the fewest Spar items.
- Keep one in: tins and cartons a dinner uses whole are chickpeas, beans, lentils, coconut milk, passata, and
  tinned or chopped tomatoes. Finishing tonight's cooking (Done on the last step) marks the ones tonight's dinner
  used as run out, so they go on the next shop.

**Non-goals**
- What a particular Spar stocks. There's no list online, since Spar shops are independent.
- Editing the Spar basics in the app. Keep it simple: the list lives in code.
- Claude inventing a dinner from the cupboard. That's maybe later.

**Approaches**
- Considered:
  - a guessed "not at the Spar" list of specialist items
  - the household ticking every ingredient
  - a short fixed list of Spar basics
- Picked the fixed list. Spar ranges change, but the basics are always there. Owner: "keep it simple".

**Left to the builder:** the exact basics list and the matching rules, with tests. No database change.
